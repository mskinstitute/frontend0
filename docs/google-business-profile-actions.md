## 5. Review Acquisition & Management Protocol

### Direct Review Link Generation
1. In GBP Manager, click **Ask for reviews**.
2. Copy the shortened review link: `https://g.page/r/.../review`.
3. Save as standard admission WhatsApp template.

### Review Collection Trigger
- After a student completes their project evaluation or receives their course certificate:
  ```text
  "Dear [Student Name], congratulations on completing your [Course Title] at MSK Institute! Your online verification ID is now live. If you enjoyed the practical lab sessions with Sumit Sir, please share your experience on Google: [GBP_REVIEW_LINK]. Your review helps aspiring coders in Shikohabad find the right career guidance!"
  ```

### Review Response Policy
- Respond to 100% of reviews within 24 hours.
- Mention the course name naturally in responses:
  *"Thank you, Priya! We are thrilled to hear that our NIELIT CCC practical mock tests helped you achieve Grade A. Wishing you the best in your career!"*
